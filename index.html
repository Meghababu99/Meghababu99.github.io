About me

From signals to scientific questions.

“Good research begins with careful observation, honest uncertainty and questions that matter.”

I am a space science researcher working at the intersection of ionospheric physics, satellite observations and machine learning. I completed my PhD in Space Science and Technology at the University of Trento and Fondazione Bruno Kessler in Italy.

My research uses computational methods to study transient geophysical phenomena. I am especially interested in building analysis that is reproducible, statistically careful and grounded in the physics of the system.

Research focus

Three threads, one connected system.

I combine physical context and data-driven methods to explore the complex links between the Sun, near-Earth space and our planet.

01

Ionosphere–Earth coupling

Studying possible statistical associations between ionospheric electric-field variations and seismic activity using satellite observations and careful controls.

Geospace · Seismo-ionosphere
02

Machine learning for geophysics

Exploring anomaly detection and deep-learning approaches for multivariate time series and spectral representations of ionospheric data.

Time series · Explainable analysis
03

Space weather & solar–terrestrial physics

Investigating solar-wind structures and transient events, and their impacts on Earth’s magnetosphere and upper atmosphere.

Solar wind · Magnetosphere

My approach connects physical questions, robust data analysis and transparent evaluation.

Observe → Model → Validate
Mentoring & outreach

Making research easier to enter.

Research can feel like a closed world when students first encounter it. Through mentoring and outreach, I want to make the process more approachable: how to ask a useful question, read scientific work, and build the skills to investigate an idea.

I work with school learners, university students and early-career researchers, connecting curiosity with practical guidance informed by my own research journey.

Curiosity is a starting point. Evidence helps it become discovery.

Topics I support

  • Scientific thinking and developing research questions
  • Reading papers and understanding research methods
  • Academic writing and research presentations
  • Preparing for PhD applications and interviews
  • Responsible use of AI and computational tools
